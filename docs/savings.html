What you save

If you run local AI on a computer you already own, you save the whole subscription. If you buy hardware, here is how long it takes to pay for itself.

The short answer: if you run local AI on a computer you already own, you save the full subscription from the first month. If you buy hardware for it, you save money only after the hardware pays for itself, and that can take years if you are replacing a $20 plan.

The $0 route

Everything in the guide above is free: Ollama, the models, Docker Desktop for personal use, and every workspace app. If your current computer has 16 GB of memory or more, you can run a useful model today, and your only costs are your setup time and a little electricity.

If you buy hardware

Months to break even is the hardware cost divided by the monthly plan you cancel. The hardware amounts below are examples, not product quotes, and they leave out electricity and your time.

ScenarioHardwarePlan replacedBreak even
Use the computer you own$0$20 a monthRight away

AI desktop$2,000$20 a month100 months

The last row is the honest one. Buying an expensive machine to replace a $20 plan does not pay off. Hardware makes sense for heavy users on the $100 and $200 plans, or for people who want privacy and no limits more than they want the savings.

What you give up

A local model is not Claude or GPT, so the saving only counts if the local model does the work you need. That is why the guide ends with a test before you cancel. If it handles your everyday tasks, keep a free cloud account for the hard ones and pocket the difference.
