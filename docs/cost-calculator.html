Frequently asked questions

How much electricity does running a local AI model actually consume?

For typical desktop use (1 to 3 hours of active generation daily), an Apple Silicon Mac adds approximately $1 to $2 per month to your electric bill. A desktop PC with a dedicated NVIDIA graphics card adds approximately $3 to $7 per month depending on your local kilowatt-hour rate.

Does hardware lose value over two years?

Yes, computer hardware depreciates over time. However, unlike subscription fees which leave you with nothing once cancelled, physical computers retain resale value and can perform general desktop tasks, gaming, or file storage.

What if I only use AI tools occasionally throughout the week?

If you only need AI assistance a few times a week, a flat $20 monthly subscription is poor value. Running a free model on your current computer costs zero dollars, while pay-as-you-go API credits typically cost less than $1 per month for light use.
