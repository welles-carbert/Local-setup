What replaces what

A paid AI plan bundles four things together: the model, the chat app, your projects, and sometimes an agent that does tasks for you. On a local setup each one is a separate free tool, and this guide installs them in order.

What you pay for nowFree local replacementGuide step
The AI model (Claude, GPT, Gemini)An open model such as Gemma, Qwen or Llama, run by OllamaSteps 3 and 4
The chat app in your browserOpen WebUI or LibreChat, which run in DockerSteps 5 to 7
Projects with instructions and filesA model preset with a system prompt and a knowledge base inside the workspace appStep 8
An agent that runs tasks (like Claude Code)Hermes Agent, a free open-source agent that can use your local modelSteps 5 and 7

Set your expectations first. A local model is not Claude or GPT. How fast and how capable it is depends on your computer's memory and the model you pick. Expect it to handle everyday writing, summarizing and questions about your own files well, and expect it to fall short on hard reasoning and very long inputs. Step 9 shows how to test this before you cancel anything.

Frequently asked questions

Can a local model really replace a $20-a-month subscription?

For drafting, rewriting, summarizing, brainstorming, and querying your own documents, yes. Modern open models run locally handle everyday desktop tasks well. Top commercial cloud models still perform better at advanced coding, multi-step math, and massive context analysis.

Do I need a high-end graphics card to run AI locally?

No. Any Apple Silicon Mac (M1 through M4) runs local models directly from unified system memory. On Windows and Linux, a dedicated NVIDIA card makes generation faster, but models will still run on your main CPU and RAM alone.

Is it completely free to run AI on my computer?

Yes. Ollama, open-weight models, Docker Desktop for personal use, and workspace apps like Open WebUI are open source or free for personal use. Your only expense is the electricity your computer uses while generating text.

Does local AI work offline without an internet connection?

Yes. Once you complete the one-time download of your chosen model and workspace app, everything processes entirely on your physical machine. You can disconnect Wi-Fi and continue chatting.

Will running local AI slow down my other applications?

While generating a reply, a local model will use processor or graphics capacity and hold its working size in memory. When idle, Ollama unloads the model from memory after five minutes by default so your machine returns to normal speed.
