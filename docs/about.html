About this site

Last updated November 6, 2026

Local Setup is a free guide to running AI on your own computer instead of paying a monthly subscription. It started with one question: how much of what people pay AI services for can a home computer do for free?

How the guides are written

  • Free first. Every page starts with what you can do with what you already own. Paid options come after and are labeled as paid.
  • Show the cost. Any claim about savings sits next to the hardware cost and the break-even math.
  • Cite the source. Install steps link to each tool's official docs, and each page shows the date it was last checked.
  • Be honest about limits. Local models are not the top paid models, and the guides say where they fall short.

How the site is paid for

The guides are free to read. The site is supported by reader donations, display ads, and affiliate links on the gear pages. Affiliate links never change what is recommended, and the affiliate disclosure explains how they work.
