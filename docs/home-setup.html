Why build your own setup

You can run everything in this guide on the computer you already have. A dedicated home setup is worth it if you use AI every day: a machine with enough memory for bigger models, a second screen so the chat sits next to your work, and a comfortable keyboard and mouse for long sessions. Start with what you own, and upgrade one piece at a time when something slows you down.

You do not need to buy anything to follow this guide. Each category below starts with the free option, then lists paid upgrades for people who want a dedicated setup. Paid items are labeled as paid.

Computers to run your models

Free

The computer you own

Any Mac with Apple Silicon or a PC with 16 GB of memory runs a useful model. Start here, and buy only if Step 9 shows you need more.

Who needs it: Everyone starting out. Who can skip upgrades: Anyone whose daily writing and summarizing tasks run smoothly on their existing machine.

Paid

Mac with unified memory (Mac mini or Studio)

On Apple Silicon, unified memory is shared between the CPU and graphics cores. Aim for 32 GB or 64 GB to run 14b and 32b models with whisper-quiet fan operation.

Who needs it: People who want a low-power, quiet desktop capable of running 30b models without heat. Who can skip it: Anyone with a functioning modern laptop.

Paid

PC with an NVIDIA graphics card

An NVIDIA GPU with 12 GB to 16 GB of dedicated VRAM provides fast token generation speeds for 8b and 14b models.

Who needs it: Users who demand the fastest token-generation speeds or who already use a Windows PC for work and gaming. Who can skip it: Mac users or anyone satisfied with standard generation speeds.

Who needs it: Users with small 256 GB laptop internal drives who want to experiment with multiple large models. Who can skip it: Users with 512 GB or larger internal drives.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Mac or a Windows PC better for running local AI at home?

Apple Silicon Macs offer unified memory, meaning a 36 GB or 64 GB Mac can run large 30b models at low power without a loud graphics card. Windows PCs with dedicated NVIDIA cards run smaller 8b to 14b models faster, but VRAM is capped by the GPU price.

Is 16 GB of RAM enough for the future?

16 GB of RAM is plenty for running efficient 7b to 9b parameter models today. If you plan to run larger 27b to 32b models or deep context analysis, 32 GB or 64 GB is recommended for future-proofing.

Can I use an AMD graphics card for local AI?

Ollama supports ROCm on select AMD Radeon GPUs on Linux and Windows. However, NVIDIA remains much simpler to set up with broader software support across open-source tools.

Why is the Mac mini so popular for local AI?

The Mac mini with 24 GB or 32 GB of unified memory offers a quiet, compact, low-power desktop that can run 14b models smoothly without the heat, noise, or electrical draw of a gaming tower.
